<a id="section-part-1-quantities-driving-forces-and-transport"></a>

## Part 1: quantities, driving forces, and transport

Define the measurement before choosing a material or a model. Learn to distinguish composition, equilibrium, kinetics, and arrival at an interface.

1. [Molecules and measurement](01-molecules-and-measurement.md): quantities, VOC terminology, concentration, and a defined measurand.
2. [Thermodynamics and redox](02-thermodynamics-and-redox.md): electrochemical potential, reference conventions, and kinetics.
3. [Transport and partitioning](03-transport-and-partitioning.md): diffusion, convection, uptake, and characteristic times.

**Practice:** [Lab 01: units](../labs/01-units-and-measurement.md) and [Lab 02: transport](../labs/02-transport-and-storage.md).

<a id="section-part-2-interfaces-materials-and-methods"></a>

## Part 2: interfaces, materials, and methods

Connect molecular interactions to observables while testing competing explanations. Treat material selection as an evidence problem.

4. [Electrode interfaces](04-electrode-interfaces.md): charging, adsorption, electrode roles, and interfacial processes.
5. [Ionic liquids and materials](05-ionic-liquids-and-materials.md): properties, water, stability, and interpretation of structural evidence.
6. [Electrochemical methods](06-electrochemical-methods.md): voltammetry, time responses, impedance, and identifiability.

**Practice:** [Lab 03: impedance](../labs/03-impedance-and-identifiability.md). **Reading:** [Foundations bibliography](../references/fundamentals.md).

<a id="section-part-3-the-integrity-of-a-sample"></a>

## Part 3: the integrity of a sample

Study transport and handling as independent sources of measurement uncertainty. The lessons provide tools for selected case studies; they do not require combining every component into one instrument.

7. [Sampling and fluidics](07-sampling-and-fluidics.md): flow, residence time, pressure drop, and representative delivery.
8. [Humidity, filtration, and conditioning](08-humidity-filtration-and-conditioning.md): water balance, particles, recovery, and controls.
9. [Sample storage and modularity](09-sample-storage-and-modularity.md): retention, contamination, interfaces, and traceability.

**Practice:** revisit [Lab 02](../labs/02-transport-and-storage.md) with the new failure mechanisms. **Reading:** [Systems bibliography](../references/systems-and-validation.md).

<a id="section-part-4-instruments-calibration-and-evidence"></a>

## Part 4: instruments, calibration, and evidence

Turn a response into a measurement with explicit operating limits. Separate analytical performance from application-level evidence.

10. [Electronics and embedded systems](10-electronics-and-embedded-systems.md): readout, noise, digitization, timing, and faults.
11. [Calibration and uncertainty](11-calibration-and-uncertainty.md): reference values, calibration, detection, and uncertainty.
12. [Breath sensing and clinical evidence](12-breath-sensing-and-clinical-evidence.md): physiological confounders and independent validation.

**Practice:** [Lab 04: calibration](../labs/04-calibration-and-uncertainty.md). **Checkpoint:** [Integrated problem set](../assessments/problem-set.md).

<a id="section-part-5-information-signals-and-learning"></a>

## Part 5: information, signals, and learning

Determine what an array can identify and what a model can legitimately generalize. Keep acquisition groups, causality, and uncertainty visible.

13. [Arrays and selectivity](13-arrays-and-selectivity.md): diversity, redundancy, correlation, and inverse problems.
14. [Signal processing and features](14-signal-processing-and-features.md): baselines, filtering, representations, and delay.
15. [Machine learning and domain shift](15-machine-learning-and-domain-shift.md): grouped evaluation, drift, calibration transfer, and unknown inputs.

**Practice:** [Lab 05: arrays](../labs/05-array-design.md) and [Lab 06: drift](../labs/06-drift-and-validation.md).

<a id="section-part-6-dynamic-sensing-research-and-independent-design"></a>

## Part 6: dynamic sensing, research, and independent design

Connect established principles to new questions. Develop a bounded project from independently documented methods and verify the selected interfaces.

16. [Olfaction and active sampling](16-olfaction-and-active-sampling.md): biological coding, plumes, timing, and sensing actions.
17. [Research frontiers](17-research-frontiers.md): recent primary studies, evidence levels, and fair comparisons.
18. [System design and responsible use](18-system-design-and-responsible-use.md): requirements, budgets, interpretation, and verification.

**Practice:** [Lab 07: timing](../labs/07-plume-and-timing.md) and [Lab 08: capstone](../labs/08-capstone-studio.md). **Next step:** [Choose a project route](../projects/index.md).
