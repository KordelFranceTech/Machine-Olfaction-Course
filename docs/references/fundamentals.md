This reading list supports Modules 01–06. Bibliographic details and linked source records were checked on 22 November 2026. It combines formal terminology, authoritative data resources, teaching references, and selected primary studies. The recent studies are examples of active research, not an exhaustive survey or a ranking of all available technologies.

The lessons contain original worked examples with explicitly hypothetical parameters. Unless a value is attributed to a cited experiment, it is a teaching value and must not be treated as measured sensor performance.

<a id="section-units-chemical-properties-and-metrology"></a>

## Units, chemical properties, and metrology

### F01. International System of Units

**Bureau International des Poids et Mesures.** *The International System of Units (SI)*, ninth edition, 2019; version revised June 2026. [Official brochure and version history](https://www.bipm.org/en/si-brochure-9). DOI: [10.59161/AUEZ1291](https://doi.org/10.59161/AUEZ1291).

**Use:** quantity definitions, unit notation, and clear expression of ratios. **Access basis:** official publication page and revision record. **Limit:** unit correctness does not establish the adequacy of a sampling or calibration procedure. **Read with:** Module 01.

### F02. International Vocabulary of Metrology

**Joint Committee for Guides in Metrology.** *International Vocabulary of Metrology: Basic and General Concepts and Associated Terms*. [Official interactive terminology, measurement chapter](https://www.iso.org/sites/JCGM/VIM/JCGM_200e_FILES/MAIN_JCGM_200e/02_e.html); [JCGM publications portal](https://www.bipm.org/en/committees/jc/jcgm/publications).

**Use:** measurand, calibration, uncertainty, and related distinctions. **Access basis:** official ISO-hosted terminology and BIPM catalogue. **Limit:** definitions are a common language; application still requires an explicit measurement model. **Read with:** Module 01 and the design requirements exercise.

### F03. Guide to measurement uncertainty

**Joint Committee for Guides in Metrology.** *Evaluation of Measurement Data: Guide to the Expression of Uncertainty in Measurement*, JCGM 100:2008, with later supporting publications and amendments listed in the official catalogue. [Publication portal](https://www.bipm.org/en/committees/jc/jcgm/publications). DOI: [10.59161/JCGM100-2008E](https://doi.org/10.59161/JCGM100-2008E).

**Use:** constructing uncertainty budgets and propagating uncertainty through a measurement model. **Access basis:** official guide and catalogue. **Limit:** simple linear propagation can be inadequate for nonlinear models or strongly non-Gaussian inputs; correlations must be considered. **Read with:** Module 01.

### F04. Fundamental physical constants

**Tiesinga, E.; Mohr, P. J.; Newell, D. B.; Taylor, B. N.** NIST database of the 2022 CODATA recommended fundamental physical constants. [Official NIST portal](https://www.nist.gov/pml/fundamental-physical-constants).

**Use:** consistent values for physical constants in numerical exercises. **Access basis:** official database description. **Limit:** precise constants do not eliminate uncertainty in measured concentration, area, temperature, or geometry. **Read with:** Modules 01–03.

### F05. NIST Chemistry WebBook

**National Institute of Standards and Technology; Linstrom, P. J., general editor.** *NIST Chemistry WebBook*, Standard Reference Database 69. [Database](https://webbook.nist.gov/chemistry/). DOI: [10.18434/T4D303](https://doi.org/10.18434/T4D303).

**Use:** identify compounds and locate thermochemical, phase-change, and spectral data with original references. **Access basis:** official database and documentation page, reporting a 2025 data update. **Limit:** inspect each datum's phase, conditions, and provenance; do not transfer pure-substance values uncritically to mixtures. **Read with:** Module 01 property-card assignment.

### F06. Meaning of VOC

**US Environmental Protection Agency.** *Technical Overview of Volatile Organic Compounds*. [Official technical overview](https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq/technical-overview-volatile-organic-compounds).

**Use:** understand why scientific and regulatory uses of “VOC” can differ. **Access basis:** official explanatory page. **Limit:** the page does not define the chemical coverage of a particular sensor or a universal human odor endpoint. **Read with:** Module 01.

## Electrochemical foundations

### F07. Electrochemical terminology

**Pingarrón, J. M.; Labuda, J.; Barek, J.; Brett, C. M. A.; Camões, M. F.; Fojta, M.; Hibbert, D. B.** “Terminology of electrochemical methods of analysis (IUPAC Recommendations 2019).” *Pure and Applied Chemistry* **92** (2020), 641–694. [Publisher record and article](https://doi.org/10.1515/pac-2018-0109).

**Use:** formal definitions of electrodes, quantities, and electroanalytical methods. **Access basis:** publisher record and publicly indexed article text. **Limit:** terminology does not validate a particular physical interpretation of data. **Read with:** Modules 02–06.

### F08. Electrochemical potential

**International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry.** “Electrochemical potential.” *Compendium of Chemical Terminology*, fifth edition, 2025. [Gold Book entry](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/E01945). DOI: [10.1351/goldbook.E01945](https://doi.org/10.1351/goldbook.E01945).

**Use:** connect chemical and electrical contributions to thermodynamic driving force. **Access basis:** official terminology entry. **Limit:** an equilibrium definition alone does not determine a reaction rate. **Read with:** Module 02.

### F09. Nernst equation

**International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry.** “Nernst equation.” *Compendium of Chemical Terminology*, fifth edition, 2025. [Gold Book entry](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/09068). DOI: [10.1351/goldbook.09068](https://doi.org/10.1351/goldbook.09068).

**Use:** reaction-quotient dependence of equilibrium potential and the distinction between standard and formal potentials. **Access basis:** official entry. **Limit:** activity, reaction stoichiometry, and reference conventions must match the problem. **Read with:** Module 02.

### F10. Electrochemical Methods

**Bard, A. J.; Faulkner, L. R.; White, H. S.** *Electrochemical Methods: Fundamentals and Applications*, third edition. Wiley, 2022. [Publisher record](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/electrochemical-methods-fundamentals-and-applications-3rd-edition-p-9781119334064); [publisher's chapter excerpt](https://catalogimages.wiley.com/images/db/pdf/9781119334064.excerpt.pdf).

**Use:** a coherent textbook progression through thermodynamics, kinetics, transport, and methods. **Access basis:** publisher metadata and available excerpt; the full book was not treated as an openly retrieved source. **Limit:** complete access may require a library or purchase. **Read with:** the entire foundations sequence.

### F11. Engineering implementation of electrochemical models

**COMSOL.** *Electrochemistry Module User's Guide*, version 6.4. [Official documentation PDF](https://doc.comsol.com/6.4/doc/com.comsol.help.echem/ElectrochemistryModuleUsersGuide.pdf).

**Use:** examples of how electrode kinetics, ionic transport, and electrical boundary conditions are coupled in an engineering model. **Access basis:** official software documentation. **Limit:** a software implementation is not independent experimental validation, and no commercial software is required for this curriculum. **Read with:** Modules 02–03.

<a id="section-transport-and-partitioning"></a>

## Transport and partitioning

### F12. Henry-law constants and conventions

**Sander, R.** “Compilation of Henry's law constants (version 5.0.0) for water as solvent.” *Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics* **23** (2023), 10901–12440. [Open article](https://acp.copernicus.org/articles/23/10901/2023/). DOI: [10.5194/acp-23-10901-2023](https://doi.org/10.5194/acp-23-10901-2023). [Maintained database](https://henrys-law.org/henry/).

**Use:** consistent definitions and access to source measurements for gas/water partitioning. **Access basis:** open article, database, and conversion documentation. **Limit:** water-solvent constants cannot be substituted for those of an ionic liquid or polymer. **Read with:** Module 03.

### F13. Cottrell equation

**International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry.** “Cottrell equation.” *Compendium of Chemical Terminology*, fifth edition, 2025. [Gold Book entry](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/09069/html). DOI: [10.1351/goldbook.09069](https://doi.org/10.1351/goldbook.09069).

**Use:** ideal planar diffusion-limited current and its explicit assumptions. **Access basis:** official entry. **Limit:** finite films, convection, early charging, and kinetic limitation can invalidate the model. **Read with:** Modules 03 and 06.

<a id="section-interfaces-and-ionic-liquid-research"></a>

## Interfaces and ionic-liquid research

### F14. Overscreening and crowding theory

**Bazant, M. Z.; Storey, B. D.; Kornyshev, A. A.** “Double Layer in Ionic Liquids: Overscreening versus Crowding.” *Physical Review Letters* **106** (2011), 046102. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.106.046102); [author-hosted article](https://web.mit.edu/bazant/www/papers/pdf/Bazant_2011_PRL_ionic_liquid_DL.pdf). Read with the [2012 erratum](https://web.mit.edu/bazant/www/papers/pdf/Bazant_2012_PRL_ionic_liquid_erratum.pdf), *Physical Review Letters* **109**, 149903.

**Use:** an influential continuum treatment of correlations and finite-size effects. **Access basis:** author-hosted paper and erratum. **Limit:** this is a model of ionic structure and capacitance, not a demonstration of target-specific vapor sensing. **Read with:** Module 04.

### F15. Water and electrochemical stability

**Chen, M.; Wu, J.; Ye, T.; Ye, J.; Zhao, C.; Bi, S.; Yan, J.; Mao, B.; Feng, G.** “Adding salt to expand voltage window of humid ionic liquids.” *Nature Communications* **11** (2020), 5809. [Open article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-19469-3). DOI: [10.1038/s41467-020-19469-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-19469-3).

**Use:** a primary example combining molecular modeling and measurements to study water's interfacial role. **Access basis:** publisher full text. **Limit:** observations apply to the investigated systems and do not establish a universal sensing recipe. **Read with:** Module 05.

### F16. Ion packing and structure-dependent quantities

**Karu, K.; Nerut, E. R.; Tao, X.; Kislenko, S. A.; Pohako-Esko, K.; Voroshylova, I. V.; Ivaništšev, V. B.** “Ionic liquid–electrode interface: Classification of ions, saturation of layers, and structure-determined potentials.” *Electrochimica Acta* **503** (2024), 144829. [DOI and publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.electacta.2024.144829); [author-institution record](https://research.lu.lv/en/publications/ionic-liquidelectrode-interface-classification-of-ions-saturation-2/).

**Use:** a recent computational comparison of interfacial packing behavior. **Access basis:** publisher abstract and institutional bibliographic record; detailed numerical methods were not independently reproduced. **Limit:** packing predictions are not equivalent to measured analytical selectivity. **Read with:** Module 05.

### F17. Imaging heterogeneous interfacial structure

**Ai, Q.; Bonagiri, L. K. S.; Panse, K. S.; Kim, J.; Zhou, S.; Zhang, Y.** “Nucleation at solid–liquid interfaces is accompanied by the reconfiguration of electrical double layers.” *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences* **122** (2025), e2421635122. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2421635122); [open full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12318177/).

**Use:** primary evidence that local interfacial structure can evolve around growing surface features in a studied ionic-liquid system. **Access basis:** full text. **Limit:** an energy-storage model interface does not directly validate a gas sensor. **Read with:** Modules 04–05.

<a id="section-measurement-methods-and-reproducibility"></a>

## Measurement methods and reproducibility

### F18. Practical cyclic voltammetry

**Elgrishi, N.; Rountree, K. J.; McCarthy, B. D.; Rountree, E. S.; Eisenhart, T. T.; Dempsey, J. L.** “A Practical Beginner's Guide to Cyclic Voltammetry.” *Journal of Chemical Education* **95** (2018), 197–206; first published online in 2017. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jchemed.7b00361); [institutional record](https://repository.lsu.edu/chemistry_pubs/323/).

**Use:** accessible measurement practice and common pitfalls. **Access basis:** institutional record and publicly accessible article copy. **Limit:** ideal educational examples require re-evaluation for thin films, gas delivery, and mixed mechanisms. **Read with:** Modules 05–06.

### F19. Impedance tutorial and correction

**Lazanas, A. Ch.; Prodromidis, M. I.** “Electrochemical Impedance Spectroscopy—A Tutorial.” *ACS Measurement Science Au* **3** (2023), 162–193. [Open article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10288619/). DOI: [10.1021/acsmeasuresciau.2c00070](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsmeasuresciau.2c00070).

**Companion correction:** same authors, *ACS Measurement Science Au* **5** (2025), 156. [Correction DOI](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsmeasuresciau.5c00007).

**Use:** phasors, circuits, spectrum presentation, and practical interpretation. **Access basis:** full tutorial and publisher correction record. **Limit:** read corrected equations; fitting a circuit does not prove its molecular interpretation. **Read with:** Modules 04 and 06.

### F20. Kramers–Kronig consistency testing

**Boukamp, B. A.** “A Linear Kronig–Kramers Transform Test for Immittance Data Validation.” *Journal of the Electrochemical Society* **142** (1995), 1885–1894. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1149/1.2044210); [author-institution record and full-text link](https://research.utwente.nl/en/publications/a-linear-kronig-kramers-transform-test-for-immittance-data-valida/).

**Use:** a foundational method for checking consistency of impedance components. **Access basis:** institutional record and author-institution PDF. **Limit:** finite bandwidth, noise, and representation choices matter; a consistency test does not uniquely identify a physical circuit. **Read with:** Module 06.

### F21. Reproducible impedance analysis software

**Murbach, M. D.; Gerwe, B.; Dawson-Elli, N.; Tsui, L.-k.** “impedance.py: A Python package for electrochemical impedance analysis.” *Journal of Open Source Software* **5** (2020), 2349. [Paper](https://joss.theoj.org/papers/10.21105/joss.02349); [maintained repository](https://github.com/ECSHackWeek/impedance.py).

**Use:** inspectable implementations for circuit fitting and validation exercises. **Access basis:** paper and official repository. **Limit:** pin the software version for reproducible work; a numerical fit remains dependent on experimental quality and model choice. **Read with:** Module 06.

<a id="section-reading-to-design-exercise"></a>

## Reading-to-design exercise

For one primary study, produce a page with five labeled statements: what the authors measured; what they simulated; which variables were controlled; what inference they made; and which inference your hypothetical sensor would still need to test. Add the answer to the [design studio](../design-studio.md). This exercise is more valuable than collecting a long list of impressive performance numbers without comparable conditions.
